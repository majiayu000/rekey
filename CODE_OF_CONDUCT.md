# Code of conduct

Participation in Rekey must be respectful, safe, and focused on the work.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, deliberate exposure of credentials or personal information, and
sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Give technical criticism with concrete evidence and address the change rather
than the person. Respect security embargoes and never pressure a reporter to
publish sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this
policy is violated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner. Do not use a
public issue when the report contains personal or security-sensitive details.
Enforcement will consider safety, severity, context, and repeated behavior.
